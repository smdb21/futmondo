# Futmondo development scope

Updated 2026-10-26.

The prediction and automation roadmap is retired. Current work follows the [data architecture redesign plan](data_architecture_redesign_plan.md), with actual results recorded in [review and implementation results](data_review_results.md).

The product covers factual player/team/market inspection, historical points and valuations, league finances and standings, official notifications, and explicit manual transactions. Predictions, forecast-driven recommendations/optimizers, Smart Bid, automated coaches, scheduled workers, and unattended trading are excluded from future development.

Historical migration files remain for existing database provenance and upgrade verification. Their presence does not authorize reinstating removed features.
