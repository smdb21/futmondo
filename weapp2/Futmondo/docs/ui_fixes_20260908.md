# Player card, login and sync fixes — 2026-09-08

The roster parser previously flattened reported match statistics into `average`, `matches` and `averageLastFive`, while the rating expected `average.average`, `average.matches` and `average.averageLastFive`. This caused roster-based player cards to miss existing match evidence. The shared normalizer now preserves consistent fields across roster, market and catalog responses. Missing averages and zero appearances still leave a rating unavailable. Coverage is the number of observed indicators out of five: 20% means one indicator, not a confidence probability. When four indicators are available but the API reports no availability status, coverage is 80% and availability says **Not reported**. No Koke-specific rating was inferred or fabricated.

Smart bids now respect both the current valuation and listing minimum, with a **No bid** result when the required amount exceeds verified funds or the valuation ceiling. New and modified bids are checked before submission. Player chart axes use independent padded ranges, including constant and zero-only series. Login displays immediate browser feedback and blocks duplicate submissions until completion.

Background sync now tolerates incomplete club enrichment and distinguishes configuration, access, schema, connection and other write failures. Retry status follows the matching operation and entity context; unrelated successful writes cannot hide failures. Queue rejections remain visible until the matching retry succeeds. Diagnostics omit credentials and raw response payloads.
